# Dashboard surface

Mode: Operate. The reviewer/creator needs to understand brand health, find the work that needs a decision, and act without hunting.

Audience: brand reviewers and content creators in a growing organisation. Frequency: daily. Task: scan health, open the queue, review content against evidence, approve or request changes. Proof: scores, issue categories, guideline references, recent actions. Constraint: synthetic demo data; no auth; must run in a browser at 1440 and 390 widths.

Chosen direction: a soft cream editorial review desk with a charcoal rail, sage as the working accent, hairline rules, and bold display type. The memorable moment is the dark "Needs your call" queue with an oversized count and a sage pulse chart that reacts to the selected period.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm editorial review desk where brand evidence and the next decision share one surface; it refuses the equal-card KPI grid and the chart-wall default.
OWN-WORLD: Warm paper cream ground, charcoal navigation rail, sage as a working accent, hairline rules and 20px operable panels; Bricolage Grotesque display over Manrope UI, tabular numerals, one organic sage pulse field. No gradients as decoration, no glass, no hard-offset shadows.
STORY: The lead understands the brand's health, sees exactly what needs a call, and reaches a decision in one pass without losing the evidence.
FIRST VIEWPORT: 264px charcoal sidebar; a slim top bar; a 44px greeting; one hairline metric strip; a 2/3 health panel with a 96px score and interactive pulse chart; a 1/3 charcoal queue panel with an oversized count and three review rows. Cream field, no hero image.
FORM: User-pinned Operate direction (soft cream + sage + charcoal), code-led; seed: none (unavailable).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
